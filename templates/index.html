              📖 How Quantum Performance Analysis Works
            

            

              Unlike classical linear models ($w_1 \cdot \text{att} + w_2 \cdot \text{study}$), quantum angle encoding and CNOT entanglement treat consistency and study time as interconnected states in a 4-dimensional Hilbert space.
            

          

        


        

          
          

            Step 1
            
Angle Encoding ($R_y$ Gate)

            

              Each student metric is normalized into an angle $\theta \in [0, \pi]$ and encoded as a rotation about the Y-axis on the Bloch sphere:
            

            

              $\theta_{\text{att}} = \frac{\text{attendance}}{100} \cdot \pi$

              $\theta_{\text{study}} = \min(\frac{\text{study}}{20}, 1) \cdot \pi$

              $R_y(\theta) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos(\theta/2) & -\sin(\theta/2) \\ \sin(\theta/2) & \cos(\theta/2) \end{pmatrix}$
            

          


          
          

            Step 2
            
Controlled-NOT Entanglement

            

              A CNOT gate is applied between Qubit 0 (Attendance) and Qubit 1 (Study). In education, study hours and attendance are correlated: attendance primes independent study and study reinforces attendance.
            

            

              $\text{CNOT} |q_0 q_1\rangle \to |q_0, q_0 \oplus q_1\rangle$

              Creates quantum entanglement between student metrics.
            

          


          
          

            Step 3
            
Superposition & Collapse

            

              Measuring the circuit collapses the wave function into one of four computational basis states: $|00\rangle, |01\rangle, |10\rangle, |11\rangle$.
            

            

              $P(\text{Pass}) = 1.0 - P('00') = 100\% - P('00')$

              State $|00\rangle$ = Ground state failure.

              Any state containing '1' = Student reached passing excitation.
            

          


          
          

            Step 4
            
Why Quantum Entanglement?

            

              Classical linear formulas assign independent weights. Quantum entanglement rewards synergy: high attendance boosts study retention, creating a non-linear probability landscape that mirrors real learning dynamics.
            

            

              Passing Threshold: $\ge 50.0\%$ Pass Probability

              Failing Threshold: $< 50.0\%$ Pass Probability
